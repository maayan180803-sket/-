# הנחיות ליצירת קליפי הרקע (שש סצנות)

ההנחיות באנגלית כי רוב כלי יצירת התמונות והוידאו עובדים טוב יותר כך. אפשר להעתיק כמו שהן.

## הנחיה משותפת (להדביק בתחילת כל הנחיה, לשמירה על אחידות)
```
Soft 3D animated style, clean matte materials, gentle rounded shapes, warm cream, soft grey and warm yellow palette, calm lighting, no text, no logos, no flags, no political symbols, no party colors (no red, blue, green). 16:9, cinematic, slow camera movement.
```

## סצנה 1 | החלטות יומיות (כ-15 שניות)
```
A cozy minimal room in warm cream and yellow tones. Quick light cuts between four close-ups: a shirt on a hanger, a plate of food, a phone with blank message bubbles on screen, a finger swiping a message aside. Playful, light, fast rhythm. Last shot calms into a steady medium view of the room. Bright flat daylight.
```

## סצנה 2 | "רגע קצת אחר" (כ-20 שניות)
```
The same room slowly empties and colors fade to muted grey-cream. Two plain doors stand side by side; the left engraved with a small blank plaque, the right the same, the right door very slightly brighter. Slow dolly-in toward the doors, then the camera holds still. Dim soft shadows, quiet and echoing atmosphere. No text readable.
```

## סצנה 3 | ילדות ומבוגרים (כ-30 שניות)
```
Low camera angle, a small child's shadow on a wall in front of tall adult figures with no faces, abstract and soft. Long side light casting long shadows. The camera slowly rises to eye level as the shadow grows taller; at the end the light evens out and the shadow becomes an adult shadow with open empty hands.
```

## סצנה 4 | הקולות והרעש (כ-30 שניות)
```
Many empty speech bubbles (no readable text) floating around a space, in neutral cream, grey and soft yellow tones. Small sharp bubbles and large round bubbles, slowly orbiting the center. The camera circles slowly. In the final seconds a single thin line of warm yellow light crosses the whole frame in a straight line and the bubbles drift back and fade, leaving only the light thread.
```

## סצנה 5 | "פעם בכמה שנים" (כ-20 שניות)
```
A small quiet ballot box, matte, not glowing, with a tiny paper slip floating above it, trembling slightly. Close-up on the slip. Then a slow pull-back revealing many soft shadows of other people standing around, each different in height and shape, faceless, no symbols. Focused soft light on the box, gentle light on the shadows. No text, no colors tied to any party.
```

## סצנה 6 | הסיום (כ-30 שניות)
```
A path being built piece by piece under a figure's feet, small cream stones appearing one after another, with a thin soft mist that stays and never disappears. Slow long pull-back, warm yellow light rising gradually but not overpowering the mist. In the last seconds the camera returns close as a paper slip is placed down gently. Calm, minimal, a long quiet breath at the end.
```

- להשאיר שוליים ריקים במרכז או בצד, כי האווטאר של HeyGen יופיע מעל הרקע.
